🚩 4 WARNING SIGNS

1. Guaranteed profits with no risk.

2. Requests for money before paying you.

3. Fake reviews and unrealistic promises.

4. No real company identity or contact details.

✓ 4 BETTER TRUST SIGNALS

1. Official government websites with verified domains.

2. Apps listed in official Google Play or Apple stores.

3. Companies with independently verified details.

4. Clear privacy, payment and refund policies.

REMEMBER: HTTPS and a professional-looking website do not guarantee that a site is safe. Always check its identity and reputation independently.

THINK FIRST. VERIFY ALWAYS.
